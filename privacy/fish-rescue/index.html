Privacy Policy

Fish Rescue: Block Puzzle, by Boss Byte. Effective 14 November 2026.

This policy explains what data the Fish Rescue app ("the app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Data stored only on your device

Your game progress, settings, coins, rescued creatures and any game in progress are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

The random install ID does not contain your name or any personal information. A new one is made if you reinstall the app.

We do not sell your data. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The services above process it under their own policies: Google, Firebase, RevenueCat.

Ads and your choices

The app shows optional rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one) and full-screen ads between levels. It never shows banner ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads). The "No Ads" purchase removes the ads between levels.

Notifications

Daily reminders are off until you turn them on in the game. They are scheduled on your device; no data is sent for them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will help delete the related data.

How long data is kept

Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, after which Firebase deletes them. Performance data is kept in aggregated form for a limited time under Firebase's own retention rules.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us to access or delete the data linked to your copy of the app by emailing us. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask you for details that help us find it (such as your device model and the date you installed the app). You can also stop all collection by uninstalling the app.
