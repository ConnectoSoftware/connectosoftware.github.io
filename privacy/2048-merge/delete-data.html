Delete your data

2048 Merge: Puzzle Levels, by BossByte.

2048 Merge has no accounts. Your game progress lives on your phone, and the app's analytics and crash data are linked only to a random install ID, not to your name or email.

Delete everything on your phone

Open your phone's Settings → Apps → 2048 Merge → Storage and tap Clear data, or uninstall the app.

This deletes your progress, best scores, hints, undo and settings, and stops all data collection. Your next install gets a new random ID.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: analytics events, user properties and crash reports linked to your install ID.

Kept: aggregated statistics that no longer identify a device.

Even without a request, analytics data is deleted after at most 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.

Ad data is held by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
