Privacy policy

Knots: Arrow Puzzle Escape · package com.bossbyte.knots · developer BossByte · last updated 20 November 2026

The short version

Knots: Arrow Puzzle Escape is made by BossByte. You don't need an account and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads and uses analytics, which collect some device data, explained below.

What stays on your phone

Levels, stars, hints, boosters, best scores and your settings are saved only on your device. We can't see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them for good.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads come from Google AdMob: between levels in the free version, and optional 'watch an ad' rewards. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in the game's Settings, under Privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's own privacy policy covers how it uses this data.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To make the game better we use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They record things like which levels you start, finish or fail, how long a level takes, which screens you open, boosters and hints used, your device model, OS version and country, and crash details. This data is tied to a random app ID, not to your name. It helps us fix crashes and balance difficulty. We never sell it.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases such as Remove Ads or hint packs are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details, only a confirmation that the purchase went through, stored on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the game and you want data removed, contact us.

Your choices and rights

You can change your ad consent in the game's Settings, or in your device settings, and stop all collection by uninstalling the game. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may ask us to access or delete analytics data linked to your app ID. Email us and we will help. Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months, then deleted automatically.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update it here and change the date at the top.
