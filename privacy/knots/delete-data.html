Delete your data

Knots: Arrow Puzzle Escape, by BossByte.

Knots has no accounts. Your game progress lives on your phone, and the app's analytics and crash data are linked only to a random install ID, not to your name or email.

Delete everything on your phone

Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Knots → Storage and tap Clear data, or uninstall the app.

This deletes your progress, progress, hints, undo and settings, and stops all data collection. Your next install gets a new random ID.

Email ebrahimali.cse.ru@gmail.com with the subject "Knots data deletion".

Tell us your phone model and roughly when you installed and last played the game, so we can find the data linked to your copy.

We delete the matching analytics data (Google Firebase Analytics) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: analytics events, user properties and crash reports linked to your install ID.

Kept: purchase records that Google Play must keep for tax and refund rules, and aggregated statistics that no longer identify a device.

Even without a request, analytics data is deleted after at most 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.

Ad data is held by Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
