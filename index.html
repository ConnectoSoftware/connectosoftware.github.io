Who We Are

A small studio, formed in 2025.

Connecto Software was founded in 2025 to work on the kind of problems most software companies skip past — pharmacy shelves, school attendance registers, grocery deliveries, shift rosters. Our team works across artificial intelligence, RMG software, video engines, web backend and frontend development, natural language processing, and computer vision, and we move between those disciplines depending on what a given business actually needs built.

Selected Work

Eleven problems, each built to fit the business that had it.

Warehouse floor, mid-count.

I. Inventory

Stock, tracked without the spreadsheet

Built for shops and small wholesalers who were still counting stock by hand. Every sale, delivery, and transfer updates the same ledger in real time, so what's on the shelf and what's in the system finally agree.

A storefront, mid-build.

II. Web

A shop window that works while you sleep

Biponon, our storefront platform, is the clearest example — a business picks a layout, lists its products, and has a working online store the same day, without touching a line of code.

A conversation, mid-reply.

III. Conversational AI

A reply before the customer puts their phone down

Built first for Messenger and WhatsApp sellers who were losing orders to slow replies. The bot answers product and price questions instantly, takes the order, and calls in a person only when the conversation actually needs one.

A retail floor, mid-afternoon.

IV. Retail Analytics

Knowing who walked in, not just what sold

Footfall, dwell time, and repeat visits, measured quietly from the shop floor itself. It gives a physical retailer the same kind of visibility an online store takes for granted — who came in, and whether they came back.

A classroom, mid-lesson.

V. Education

The administrative half of running a school

Attendance, results, fees, and parent communication, handled in one system built for school offices rather than software companies. What used to take a register and three separate ledgers now takes one login.

An entrance, under watch.

VI. Access & Attendance

Clocking in without the queue or the card

A camera at the door replaces the register and the ID card both. Staff are recognized and logged the moment they walk in, and the record feeds straight into payroll instead of waiting for someone to type it up.

A shift, mid-check.

VII. Workforce & Payroll

Payroll that doesn't depend on a paper timesheet

Built for businesses whose staff aren't sitting at a desk — warehouse crews, delivery teams, shift workers. Attendance is logged from wherever the work happens, and payroll is calculated from that record directly.

A dispensary, mid-shelf.

VIII. Pharmacy

Nothing expires quietly on the shelf anymore

Every batch is tracked from the day it arrives, with alerts well before anything nears its expiry date. A pharmacist spends less time auditing shelves and more time at the counter.

A consultation, mid-note.

IX. Healthcare

Patients who don't fall through the cracks

A simple system for small clinics to track who needs a follow-up call, a test result, or a repeat prescription, and when. Nothing depends on someone remembering to check a paper file.

A building, from street level.

X. Property

Running property you can't visit every week

Built for owners managing property at a distance, often with more than one party involved in a decision. Leases, payments, and maintenance requests are logged in one record instead of scattered across calls and messages.

A delivery, mid-unload.

XI. Supply & Forecasting

Ordering stock before you run short, not after

Built for grocers who order from several suppliers on different schedules. It tracks what's moving, forecasts what's about to run low, and times the next order before the shelf actually goes bare.
