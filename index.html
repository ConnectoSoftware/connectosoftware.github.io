Here's some of what we've built — and who it was built for.

Inventory management

A wholesaler we worked with was still counting stock by hand at closing time, and the numbers never quite matched what was on the shelf. We built them a system where every sale and delivery updates the same ledger automatically.

Websites and storefronts

A seller wanted to take orders beyond Messenger but had no way to put a catalogue online without hiring a developer. We built Biponon so they could list products and have a working store the same afternoon.

AI chatbots for Messenger and WhatsApp

A business was getting messages on Facebook and WhatsApp all day, but didn't have enough people to reply to every one in time. We built a chatbot that answers instantly, takes the order, and only calls a person in when the conversation really needs one.

Customer tracking for retail stores

A store owner could tell you what sold, but never who walked in and left without buying. We built a quiet system that tracks footfall and repeat visits, so a physical shop can finally see what an online store sees.

School management

A school office was running attendance, fees, and parent calls out of three different registers. We built them one system that replaced all three with a single login.

Digital attendance with face recognition

A factory was losing twenty minutes every morning to a queue at the card reader. We built a camera that recognizes staff at the door and logs them straight into payroll.

HR, attendance, and payroll for field and shift staff

A business with delivery and warehouse staff had no clean way to calculate payroll without a stack of paper timesheets. We built attendance tracking that works from wherever the work actually happens.

Pharmacy inventory and expiry tracking

A pharmacist was manually checking expiry dates shelf by shelf, every week. We built batch-level tracking that flags anything nearing its expiry before it becomes a loss.

Clinic follow-up

A small clinic kept losing track of which patients needed a follow-up call or a repeat prescription. We built them a simple list that doesn't depend on anyone remembering to check a paper file.

Property management

An owner managing a building from another city had leases, payments, and repair requests scattered across calls and texts. We built one shared record for all of it.

Grocery supplier delivery and demand forecasting

A grocer ordering from several suppliers on different schedules kept either running out of stock or over-ordering. We built forecasting that times the next order before the shelf goes bare.
